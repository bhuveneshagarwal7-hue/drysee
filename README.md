# drysee — Purely Packed. Joyfully Shared. ☀️

> Premium handpicked dry fruits, nuts, berries, and super seeds delivered across India with sunshine crunch.

Live Website: **[https://drysee.in/](https://drysee.in/)** (also mirrors on [GitHub Pages](https://bhuveneshagarwal7-hue.github.io/drysee/))

## ✨ Features
- **Artisanal Food Photography**: High-resolution editorial imagery for all 10 harvest products and a signature hero visual featuring roasted watermelon seeds, pumpkin seeds, and premium dry fruits.
- **Interactive Nutrition Facts Modal**: Scientific macro breakdowns (protein, healthy fats, fiber, vitamins) and key wellness benefits for every nut and seed (including AAA Green Pumpkin Seeds & Slow-Roasted Watermelon Seeds).
- **Dual-Tier Category & Mood Filtering**: Filter by Dry Fruits & Nuts, Super Seeds, or by snack mood (Energy Boost, Mindful Crunch, Royal Indulgence, Daily Immunity).
- **Dynamic Multi-Weight Pack Selector**: Instant switching between 250g, 500g, and 1kg with real-time price calculation.
- **Interactive Shopping Bag**: Slide-out cart drawer with free shipping progress meter (orders over ₹999).
- **Brand Identity**: Custom editorial botanical branding (Concept 2).
